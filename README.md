# Harmonic Field

A generative drone instrument built from one root note and its natural overtones, with a visual field that reacts to the sound.

- **Harmonic Field** (`/`): the simple version. 5 moods, Journey, Sleep timer, Pause, Volume and Tone. Keyboard: 1–8 for the buttons, - and = for volume, [ and ] for tone.
- **Studio** (`/studio/`): the performance version. 16 pads (scenes, layers, loops, drone moves plus Shift), 8 knobs, MIDI, and a Screen window for a second display.
- **Sky Drone** (`/sky/`): the drone played by the live weather over Carbondale, CO. Pressure bends the root, wind sets the pulse, clouds darken the tone, evening lowers everything. Weather from Open-Meteo; manual dials if the feed is down.

Everything runs in the browser. There's no AI inside, no tracking, and no accounts.
